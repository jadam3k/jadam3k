### About me

> I'm a student training at a technical secondary school in the field of automation technician. I design PCBs and I'm learning to program PLC controllers in Ladder (LAD) language. I mainly build PCBs related to industrial automation, smart home and IoT — and sometimes I branch out into drones and motor control. I work in CodeSys and KiCad, as well as Visual Studio Code, Arduino IDE and STM32CubeMX for my daily work and learning. I can also use software like Scilab/MATLAB. My goal is simple: create clean schematics and code, gain knowledge, and help people. I also play football as an amateur and I'm interested in liturgy.
